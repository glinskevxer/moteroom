# Changelog

## 1.0.0

A season can be worked end to end and closed.

- Receiving: module tickets, moisture, the yard's running order, scale
  certificates, module freight, the weighbridge csv in and out
- Ginning: lots off their own tickets, turnout, plant time and stoppages,
  drier settings, energy per bale, motes and gin trash
- Press: bale numbering with a check digit, moisture restoration off the dry
  fibre, bagging and ties against what is still to gin
- Classing: the office's fixed width transfer, colour off the chart, leaf and
  extraneous matter, sampling, re-class requests, line calibration
- Loan: the premium and discount schedule, pool value, redemption at the world
  price when the price fell under the loan
- Warehouse: receipts, storage charges, shipping orders, electronic transfers,
  movements between houses, insurance exposure, aging
- Settlement: lint allocated on dry weight, seed credit, gin charges, freight
  and byproducts, contracts, patronage and equity, cheques, deferrals, late
  accounts
- The book: sqlite with thirteen migrations, an audit trail, backup and verify,
  compaction, export
- Twenty reports, all of them data rather than text, all of them available as
  csv
- Fourteen gates the build runs against the repository itself

## 0.1.0

First working season end to end: tickets in at the scale, lots through the
plant, bales off the press, cards back from the office, receipts at the
warehouse and a settlement for the producer.
